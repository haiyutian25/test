  • Mar 23, 2026

    Saved answers and collections

    Your team can now save any Powder response as a verified answer and organize it into collections. Think of it as a living knowledge base that builds itself as your team works.
  • Mar 4, 2026

    Source attribution v2

    We rebuilt how Powder cites its answers. Every response now links to the exact paragraph, row, or message it pulled from, not just the document.

    Team permissions and roles

    Workspaces now support granular role-based access. Assign members as Admins, Editors, or Viewers to control who can connect sources, trigger actions, and manage billing.

    Workspace actions

    Powder can now execute actions directly inside your connected tools. Create a Jira ticket, update a HubSpot deal, send a Slack message, or log a note in Notion.

FAQ

Answers to the questions that come up most.

Learn how Powder works, what it connects to, how actions are handled, and what teams can expect day to day.

What is Powder and how does it work?

Powder is an AI agent that connects to your existing docs and tools to deliver verified answers, draft responses, and trigger actions directly from a chat interface. Ask a question, get a sourced answer, and execute next steps without switching tabs.

How long does it take to set up?

Most teams are up and running in under five minutes. Connect your data sources, invite your team, and Powder starts indexing immediately. No engineering resources required.

Do I need technical knowledge to use Powder?

No. Powder is designed for operators, support leads, and sales teams. The interface is conversational, and connecting integrations takes a few clicks.

Is there a free plan available?

Powder offers a free tier with limited integrations and message volume so you can evaluate the product before committing. No credit card required.

What's included in the Pro plan?

Pro unlocks unlimited integrations, approval gates, audit logs, priority support, and advanced analytics. Pricing scales with workspace seats.

Can I switch plans or cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are billed monthly or annually with no lock-in. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your workspace settings at any time.
