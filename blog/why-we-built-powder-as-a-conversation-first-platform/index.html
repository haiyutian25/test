Why We Built Powder as a Conversation-First Platform

Jan 24, 2026

Every tool we used added a tab

The original prototype of Powder was internal. We were losing hours a day to the same loop: open a tab, search, copy an ID, open another tab, paste, repeat. Every tool in that loop was good. The loop was the problem.

Interfaces should collapse, not multiply

Dashboards are great at showing state. Conversations are better at getting work done, because they let you ask the messy follow-up questions you would never bother to file a ticket for.

Conversation-first does not mean chat-only

Powder keeps your tools exactly where they are. It sits on top as the layer that makes them usable: answers come from your stack, actions execute in your stack, and every step is auditable.
